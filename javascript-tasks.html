JavaScript Tasks

Practice Task 4. Fill in the fields, press the button and see the result of each program. You can enter decimals with a dot or a comma.

1

Username

Enter a username and show it on the page.

2

Wage Calculator

Payment = hours × hourly rate. Example: 35 hours and rate 2.75 give 96.25.

3

Celsius to Fahrenheit

F = C × 9 / 5 + 32. Example: 25 °C is 77 °F.

4

Overtime Wage Calculator

Hours 41–60 are paid 1.5 times the rate, hours above 60 are paid 2 times. Example: 45 hours, rate 10 give 475.

5

Sum of Odd Numbers

Calculate the sum of odd numbers from 1 to 100 with a while loop.

6

Sum of Even Numbers

Calculate the sum of even numbers from 1 to 100 with a for loop.

7

Array: Maximum, Minimum, Average

Array: [10, 20, 30, 40, 50]

The results are shown in a message box with alert().

8

Unique Words Sorting

Given text:

But soft what light through yonder window breaks
It is the east and Juliet is the sun
Arise fair sun and kill the envious moon
Who is already sick and pale with grief

Split the text into words, keep every word only once (in lower case) and sort the list alphabetically.
